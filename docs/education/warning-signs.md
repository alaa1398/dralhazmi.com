## Sections
### Green: normal in the first weeks
- Mild pain at the small cuts, easing day by day — [expert-opinion]
- Feeling tired for a few weeks — [expert-opinion]
- Feeling full after a few spoonfuls — [expert-opinion]

### Amber: contact your surgical team today
- Nausea or vomiting that keeps coming back — [guideline, R7; R6]
- Cannot keep fluids or vitamins down for [CHAIR] hours — [expert-opinion; R6]
- Drying out: dark or little urine, dizzy when standing — [expert-opinion]
- Wound red, swollen, warm, or leaking — [expert-opinion]
- Temperature of [CHAIR] °C — [CHAIR]
- New trouble swallowing food you managed before — [expert-opinion]
- Cannot reach your team today? Go to the emergency department — [expert-opinion]

### Red: call 997 or go to the emergency department now
- Heart racing at rest, over [CHAIR] beats a minute — [CHAIR; R2, R3]
- Severe stomach pain, or pain that keeps getting worse — [expert-opinion; R4]
- Short of breath or breathing fast — [expert-opinion; R3, R4 (leak cohorts); R2, R5 not verified]
- Any chest pain — [expert-opinion; R5 not verified]
- One leg swollen, painful, or warm — [expert-opinion; R1 (clots are a main risk); R5 not verified]
- Vomiting blood, or black, tar-like stools — [expert-opinion; R14]
- Temperature of [CHAIR] °C or shaking chills — [CHAIR]
- Confusion, hard to wake, or fainting — [guideline, R7 (confusion); expert-opinion (fainting)]
- Tell staff: "I had a sleeve (or bypass) on this date." — [expert-opinion; R4]

### Later warning signs (months or years after)
- Vomiting for days plus confusion, unsteady walking, or double vision: emergency (low vitamin B1) — [guideline, R7]
- Numbness or weakness with poor eating: contact your team today — [guideline, R7]
- Bypass: sudden, strong cramping pain that comes and goes: emergency (bowel twisted through a gap, "internal hernia") — [guideline, R8, R9; action expert-opinion]
- Sleeve: new or worse heartburn, or food coming back up: tell your team — [guideline, R10]
- Within 1 hour of sweet food: sweating, racing heart, cramps, diarrhea ("dumping"): tell your team — [consensus, R11]
- 1–3 hours after eating: shaky, sweaty, hungry, confused (low blood sugar); more common after bypass: tell your team — [consensus, R11, R12]
- Low sugar with fainting or confusion: call 997 — [expert-opinion; R12]
