# Diet stages guide: web validation

Date: 2026-11-27. Purpose: check the diet stages guide (version 0.2) and its open decisions D1 to D4 against current published sources. Source: cloud session, Tavily web search and extract (approved by the owner) plus PubMed. Through Tavily, three documents Ali could not open on 2026-11-25 were read: the AACE/TOS/ASMBS 2019 guideline, the Saudi SCFHS bariatric nutrition pathway, and the OMA 2025 diet table.

## Summary

- **Every item on the guide is supported.** None is wrong.
- **The Saudi SCFHS pathway is now confirmed** and gives a slower schedule than the guide's OMA timings: solid food from about week 7, against week 4–6 in OMA. This is the main fact for D1.
- **D3 changes:** the "wait 30 minutes after eating before drinking" rule is in the AACE 2019 guideline as general advice for all patients, not only for dumping. Ali had read it as dumping-only advice from the dumping consensus.
- **Two small gaps** against the AACE "avoid or delay" list: fruit juice, and nuts and popcorn are not on the guide (suggestion below).

## Item by item

| Item on the guide | What the sources say | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Clear liquids, first 1–2 days | OMA 2025: "Post-op day 0 and 1"; "should not last longer than 48 hours" [S3]. SCFHS: clear liquids for 24–48 hours [S2]. | Confirmed by both. |
| Full liquids, high-protein and low-sugar, up to week 1–2 | OMA 2025: "Post-op day 2 through 7–14 days"; "high in protein, low in added sugar, lactose-free" [S3]. SCFHS: liquid diet 3–7 days [S2]. | Confirmed (SCFHS is shorter). |
| Puree, then soft from week 2–3 | OMA 2025: "A soft food stage typically begins 2–3 weeks after surgery" [S3]. SCFHS: puree for 1–2 weeks starting at week 3, then soft for about 2 weeks [S2]. | OMA confirmed; SCFHS starts soft later. |
| Regular textures from week 4–6 | OMA 2025 (Table 11, read by Ali). SCFHS: "Solid ... Approximately 1 month, Week 7 after surgery", then balanced diet indefinitely [S2]. | OMA confirmed; **SCFHS says week 7** (see D1). |
| Try one new food at a time | Johns Hopkins: "Try only 1 new food at a meal"; Mercy: one new food per day [S5, S6]. | Supported (program practice). |
| At least 60 g protein a day (D2) | AACE 2019: "A minimal protein intake of 60 g/d and up to 1.5 g/kg ideal body weight per day should be adequate" (Grade D) [S1]. OMA 2025 quotes the same ASMBS figure [S3]. Programs: 60–80 g (Johns Hopkins), 60–90 g (WakeMed) [S5, S7]. | Confirmed. |
| Eat protein first | Johns Hopkins, Mercy, Vanderbilt, WakeMed all say protein first [S5, S6, S7, S8]. Not found in a guideline. | Supported as practice; the "Expert opinion" tag is right. |
| Spread protein; eggs, fish, chicken, yogurt, cheese, lentils; lactose-free milk | OMA 2025: full liquids "lactose-free"; high-protein foods at meals [S3]. | Confirmed. |
| Sip all day, between meals | SCFHS: "sipping liquids very slowly"; "drinking fluids more frequently and in-between meals" [S2]. OMA 2025: sip through the day [S3]. | Confirmed, now including the Saudi pathway. |
| Don't drink with meals; wait 30 minutes after eating (D3) | AACE 2019 Table 10: "Do not drink liquids during meals" and "Wait 30 minutes after eating before resuming fluid intake" [S1]. Johns Hopkins, Vanderbilt, WakeMed use the same 30-minute rule [S5, S7, S8]. | **Confirmed as a guideline item for all patients.** |
| 6–8 cups (about 1.4–1.9 L) of fluid a day (D4) | OMA 2025: 48–64 oz a day [S3]. AACE 2019 Table 9: "maintain adequate hydration (usually >1.5 L/d PO)" [S1]. Mercy: 6–8 cups; Hopkins and WakeMed: 64 oz [S6, S5, S7]. | Confirmed; both D4 options stated correctly. |
| Limit caffeine early on | AACE 2019 lists caffeine under "Avoid/Delay" [S1]. OMA: limit or avoid caffeine [S3]. | Confirmed. |
| Small meals at set times; small bites; chew well; 20–30 minutes a meal; stop when comfortable | AACE 2019: multiple small meals, chew thoroughly, eat slowly, stop at satiety [S1]. 20–30 minutes: Mercy and WakeMed [S6, S7] (OMA per Ali). | Confirmed. |
| Avoid early: fizzy drinks; sugary drinks and sweets; tough or dry meat; bread, rice, pasta; skins and stringy vegetables | AACE 2019 "Avoid/Delay": concentrated sweets, carbonated beverages, fruit juice, fried foods, "soft doughy bread, pasta, rice", "tough, dry, red meat", "nuts, popcorn, other fibrous foods", caffeine, alcohol; "Avoid bread, rice and pasta until able to comfortably consume adequate protein, vegetables and fruits" [S1]. OMA: avoid skins, peels and fibrous foods [S3]. | Confirmed. **Not on the guide: fruit juice; nuts and popcorn.** |
| Dumping after sweets happens after either operation, more often after bypass | AACE 2019: eliminate concentrated sweets after bypass for dumping, and after any procedure [S1]. Study of 127 patients: dumping in 15.6% after sleeve and 56.4% after bypass [S4]. A review reports up to 40% after either [S9]. | Confirmed. |
| Contact the team: repeated vomiting, dehydration, food stuck, dumping not fixed by diet, before Ramadan fasting | Unchanged from Ali's review (OMA, ERAS, dumping consensus, Ramadan Delphi). | Not rechecked; no conflict found. |
| Emergency: bright red or dark brown vomit; cannot keep any fluids down; severe belly pain, chest pain, trouble breathing | Consistent with the warning-signs validation. | Supported. |

## Bearing on open decisions

| Decision | Finding |
|---|---|
| D1 (stage timing) | OMA 2025: clear day 0–1, full liquids to day 7–14, soft from week 2–3, regular from week 4–6 [S3]. **Saudi SCFHS 2020: clear 24–48 h, liquids 3–7 days, puree 1–2 weeks (from week 3), soft about 2 weeks, solid from about week 7, then balanced** [S2]. The SCFHS table has a gap: liquids last about a week but puree starts at week 3. The Saudi pathway is the national reference; OMA is newer and faster. |
| D2 (protein) | AACE 2019 primary text: at least 60 g, up to 1.5 g/kg ideal weight [S1]. US programs often give 60–80 or 60–90 g [S5, S7]. |
| D3 (drinks and meals) | AACE 2019: no drinks with meals, and wait 30 minutes after eating, for everyone [S1]. Options b) and the 30-minute wait are compatible: the guide can keep both lines. |
| D4 (fluids) | 48–64 oz (OMA) and more than 1.5 L (AACE) overlap: 6–8 cups is about 1.4–1.9 L [S1, S3]. |

## Suggested changes (not made; for your approval)

1. Retag "After eating, wait 30 minutes before drinking" as Guideline with AACE 2019 as its source, instead of the dumping consensus.
2. Add "fruit juice" to the sugary-drinks line ("Sugary drinks, fruit juice, sweets and baked goods") and add a line "Nuts and popcorn" to "Avoid early on" [S1].
3. Add the SCFHS pathway as a source, whichever schedule you choose for D1 (it also gives the pre-surgery diet as 2–6 weeks, relevant to decision P1 on the preparing guide).
