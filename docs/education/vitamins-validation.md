# Vitamins guide: web validation

Date: 2026-11-27. Purpose: check the vitamins guide (version 0.2) and its open decisions V1 to V8 against current published sources. Source: cloud session, Tavily web search and extract (approved by the owner) plus PubMed. Through Tavily, the full ASMBS 2016 micronutrient guideline and a copy of the AACE/TOS/ASMBS 2019 guideline could be read; Ali could not open either on 2026-11-25.

## Summary

- **Most of the guide is confirmed**, now against the primary guideline text, not quotes of it.
- **Two items are wrong and should change:** the selenium line and the vitamin A line both present as bypass-only things that the guidelines apply to sleeve and bypass alike (details below).
- **Two decisions get firmer facts:** V1 (AACE 2019 does say 2 multivitamins a day after sleeve or bypass) and V4 (the decision sheet misstated ASMBS on iron; corrected there).

## Item by item

| Item on the guide | What the sources say | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Complete multivitamin with minerals every day, for life | AACE 2019 R39: supplementation after sleeve and bypass "as 2 adult multivitamins plus minerals (each containing iron, folic acid, and thiamine)" (Grade B) [S2]. BOMSS 2020: a complete multivitamin and mineral supplement after all procedures [S3]. BOMSS GP guidance: "continued for life" [S4]. | Confirmed. |
| How many a day: your team will tell you (V1) | AACE 2019 Table 9: 2 tablets after sleeve and after bypass, 1 after a band [S2]. BOMSS: 1 complete tablet, doubled if an over-the-counter product lacks enough zinc and copper [S4]. UC Davis gives 2 a day, cutting to 1 at 6 months after sleeve if labs allow (practice, not guideline) [S8]. | Item is accurate. **V1 now has a confirmed primary source for "2 a day".** |
| Chewable or liquid early on | AACE 2019 R39: "should be in chewable form initially" [S2]. Johns Hopkins: chewable for at least the first 3 months [S6]. | Confirmed (AACE now cited directly, not only through OMA). |
| 1,200–1,500 mg calcium a day, food plus supplements | ASMBS 2016: "LAGB, SG, RYGB: 1200–1500 mg/d" [S1]. AACE 2019 Table 9 the same, "as calcium citrate" [S2]. | Confirmed. |
| Split calcium into smaller doses | ASMBS 2016: "Calcium should be given in divided doses" [S1]. Hopkins: 500–600 mg 2 to 3 times a day [S6]. | Confirmed. |
| Citrate with or without food; carbonate with meals (V5) | AACE 2019 (quoting ASMBS): "Calcium carbonate should be taken with meals. Calcium citrate may be taken with or without meals" [S2]. BOMSS GP guidance: citrate preferred if there is a personal or family history of kidney stones [S4]. Hopkins and AACE Table 9 name citrate [S6, S2]. | Confirmed. |
| Vitamin D daily, dose guided by blood test (V2) | ASMBS 2016: "3000 IU daily, until blood levels of 25(OH)D are greater than sufficient (30 ng/mL)" [S1]. BOMSS 2020: "Maintenance levels of between 2000 and 4000 IU oral vitamin D3 per day may be required following SG and RYGB" [S3]. | Confirmed; V2 options stated correctly. |
| Many people need extra iron (V4) | ASMBS 2016: low-risk patients ("males and patients without history of anemia") at least 18 mg from the multivitamin; "Menstruating females and patients who have undergone RYGB, SG, or BPD/DS should take at least 45–60 mg of elemental iron daily (cumulatively, including iron from all vitamin and mineral supplements)" [S1]. BOMSS 2020: after SG and RYGB "additional elemental iron is required"; menstruating women 50–100 mg a day [S3]. | Item accurate. **The decision sheet had described the US position as "only higher-risk patients and women with periods"; ASMBS also names everyone after sleeve or bypass. Corrected.** |
| Women with periods usually need more iron | ASMBS 2016 and BOMSS 2020 [S1, S3]. | Confirmed. |
| Take iron with orange juice or vitamin C | BOMSS 2020: "Taking iron supplements alongside citrus fruits/drinks or vitamin C may aid absorption" [S3]. | Confirmed. |
| Keep iron and calcium at least 2 hours apart | BOMSS 2020: "1–2 h apart" [S3]. ASMBS 2016: iron "separately from calcium supplements" (no interval) [S1]. Johns Hopkins, Kaiser Permanente, UC Davis: 2 hours [S6, S7, S8]. | Confirmed; "at least 2 hours" is the cautious end of the range. |
| Extra vitamin B12: injection or daily tablet (V3) | ASMBS 2016: by mouth 350–500 µg daily, or 1,000 µg injection monthly [S1]. BOMSS 2020: injection every 3 months after SG and RYGB [S3]. | Confirmed; V3 options stated correctly. |
| Multivitamin must contain vitamin B1 | ASMBS 2016: "at least 12 mg thiamin daily (Grade C) and preferably a 50 mg dose... once or twice daily (Grade D)" [S1]. BOMSS 2020: multivitamin containing thiamine [S3]. | Confirmed. |
| B1 can drop fast with vomiting; some teams add extra B1 for 3–4 months (V6) | BOMSS: prolonged vomiting needs extra thiamine (200–300 mg daily) and urgent referral; symptoms need admission for IV thiamine [S5]. | Confirmed. |
| Most advice is the same for both operations | AACE 2019 Table 9: identical columns for sleeve and bypass [S2]. ASMBS 2016 iron and calcium: same for SG and RYGB [S1]. | Confirmed. |
| **After bypass, selenium (a mineral) is also checked** | BOMSS 2020: check selenium "if there is chronic diarrhoea, metabolic bone disease, unexplained anaemia or unexplained cardiomyopathy" [S3]. BOMSS GP guidance lists selenium in yearly monitoring after **both** sleeve and bypass [S4]. Neither makes it bypass-only. | **Inaccurate. Change.** |
| **After bypass, some people need extra vitamin A** | ASMBS 2016 table: vitamin A 5,000–10,000 IU a day after **SG and RYGB alike** (10,000 after duodenal switch) [S1]. | **Inaccurate. Change.** |
| Blood tests at about 3, 6 and 12 months, then yearly (V7) | BOMSS 2020: "3, 6 and 12 months in the first year and at least annually thereafter" for folate, B12, vitamin D and calcium [S3]. | Confirmed. |
| Blood count, ferritin, folate, B12; vitamin D, calcium, kidney and liver | BOMSS 2020 summary [S3, S9]. | Confirmed. |
| Zinc and copper at least once a year | BOMSS 2020: "Consider monitoring serum copper levels at least annually following SG, RYGB" [S3]; BOMSS GP table lists zinc and copper yearly after sleeve and bypass [S4]. | Confirmed. |
| Hair loss or change in taste (V8) | BOMSS 2020: check zinc "if unexplained anaemia, hair loss or changes in taste acuity" [S3]. | Confirmed; V8 unchanged. |
| Emergency signs (confusion, unsteady walking, fast heartbeat with breathlessness or swollen legs) | Not rechecked here; the warning-signs validation confirmed confusion and unsteady walking as vitamin B1 emergency signs. | Unchanged. |

## Suggested changes (not made; for your approval)

1. Replace "After bypass, selenium (a mineral) is also checked" with **"Zinc, copper and selenium are checked if you have symptoms, or yearly as your team decides."** (BOMSS 2020 and GP guidance [S3, S4]). Or drop the line.
2. Replace "After bypass, some people need extra vitamin A" with **"Your multivitamin should include vitamin A."** (ASMBS 2016 [S1]). Or drop the line.
3. Cite AACE 2019 directly (R39 and Table 9) for the chewable and multivitamin lines, now that its text was read.
4. Decide V1 knowing AACE 2019 says 2 a day after sleeve or bypass.
