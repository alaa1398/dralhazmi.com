# Getting ready for surgery guide: web validation

Date: 2026-11-27. Purpose: check the preparing guide (version 0.2) and its open decisions P1 to P6 against current published sources. Source: cloud session, Tavily web search and extract (approved by the owner). Through Tavily, the AACE/TOS/ASMBS 2019 guideline could be read; Ali could not open it on 2026-11-25, so it was left off the public guide.

## Summary

- **Every item on the guide is supported.** None is wrong.
- **AACE 2019 can go back on the guide as a source.** It confirms the 12–18 month pregnancy wait, stopping estrogen before surgery, improved fertility after surgery, sleep apnea screening, and CPAP use after surgery.
- **Smoking (P2):** the US sources ask for at least 6 weeks; ERAS asks for at least 4. AACE 2019 adds that smokers should stop at any time, even within 6 weeks of surgery.
- **GLP-1 medicines (P3):** four current documents agree that most patients continue the medicine. They differ on who needs a 24-hour liquid diet. The guide's line holds under all of them.
- **Endoscopy (P5):** IFSO 2025 says endoscopy "should be strongly considered" before surgery for everyone, because symptoms do not predict what it finds.
- **Sweet clear drinks (P6):** ASA 2023 strongly recommends them up to 2 hours before surgery for healthy adults, with care in diabetes. ERAS 2021 found too little evidence in bariatric patients.

## Item by item

| Item on the guide | What the sources say | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| See the surgeon, dietitian and psychologist; blood tests | ERAS 2021 (read by Ali). IFSO-WGO 2022: psychosocial preparation and correction of deficiencies before surgery [S6]. | Supported. |
| Answer sleep apnea questions | AACE 2019 R22: "clinical screening for obstructive sleep apnea (with confirmatory polysomnography if screening tests are positive) should be considered" (Grade C) [S1]. | Confirmed. |
| You may need a camera test of the stomach (P5) | IFSO 2025: "As symptoms are not an accurate predictor of pre-MBS pathology and/or susceptibility to post-MBS GERD, upper gastrointestinal endoscopy should be strongly considered prior to MBS" [S5]. Pre-surgery scopes were abnormal in 61%; under 1% had a finding that ruled out surgery; 23% changed the planned operation [S5]. | Supported. "May need" is softer than IFSO's "strongly considered" (see P5). |
| Stop all smoking at least 4 weeks before (P2) | ERAS 2021: at least 4 weeks (Ali). ASMBS 2021 optimization statement: "Refrain from smoking for at least six weeks preoperatively" (search excerpt) [S3]. AACE 2013: "preferably at least 6 weeks" (Grade A) [S4]. AACE 2019 R23: "All smokers must be advised to stop smoking at any time before bariatric surgery, even if it is within 6 weeks before surgery" [S1]. | Supported; US sources prefer 6 weeks (see P2). |
| Smoking raises risk of ulcers, infection, lung problems | AACE 2013: marginal ulcer, pneumonia, poor wound healing [S4]. AACE 2019: smoking was the only predictor of airway complications in a 12,062-patient cohort [S1]. | Confirmed. |
| Stop vaping and all nicotine products | IFSO-WGO 2022: "Cessation of tobacco, alcohol and drugs is mandatory" [S6]. ASMBS patient FAQ: programs require stopping "all nicotine products" (Ali). | Supported; "Expert opinion" tag is fair. |
| Low-calorie diet for 2–4 weeks; shrinks the liver (P1) | ERAS 2021: 2–4 weeks (Ali). Saudi SCFHS: 2–6 weeks, 800–1,500 kcal a day, up to 3 months if assessed (diet stages validation). ASMBS FAQ: usually 2–3 weeks (Ali). | Supported. |
| Diabetes: sugar can drop; medicines may need new doses | ERAS 2021 (Ali). | Not rechecked; no conflict found. |
| Keep active, e.g. daily walks | IFSO-WGO 2022: assess and instruct patients in an exercise program before surgery [S6]. | Supported. |
| Blood thinners: never stop on your own | Expert opinion; no conflict found. | Unchanged. |
| GLP-1 injections: ask early; you may need a liquid diet first (P3) | Multi-society 2024 (ASA, AGA, ASMBS, ISPCOP, SAGES): "Most patients should continue taking their GLP-1 receptor agonists before elective surgery"; those at highest risk "should follow a liquid diet for 24 hours before the procedure" [S2]. SPAQI 2025 (endorsed by AACE): continue, with 24 hours of clear liquids and no solids for everyone without significant GI symptoms [S7]. UK 2025 consensus: continue without changing fasting (secondary summary) [S8]. | Supported under every current document. |
| Herbal products and supplements: tell your team | SPAQI 2021 (Ali). | Unchanged. |
| Hormone pills raise clot risk; your team may pause them | AACE 2019 R18: "Estrogen therapy should be discontinued before a bariatric procedure (1 cycle of oral contraceptives in premenopausal women; 3 weeks of hormone replacement therapy in postmenopausal women)" (Grade D) [S1]. | **Confirmed by the guideline**, which is firmer than "may pause". |
| Avoid pregnancy for 12–18 months (P4) | AACE 2019 R17: "avoid pregnancy pre-procedure and for 12 to 18 months post-procedure" (Grade D) [S1]. Pregnancy consensus 2019: until weight is stable, typically 1 year (Ali). | **Confirmed by the guideline.** |
| Fertility often rises after surgery | AACE 2019 R19: "Women should be advised that their fertility status might be improved after a bariatric procedure" [S1]. | Confirmed. |
| Pills may work less well; ask about an implant or IUD | AACE 2019: after bypass, counsel about non-oral contraception [S1]. Pregnancy consensus 2019: implant or IUD first choice after either operation (Ali). | Confirmed. |
| Last light meal 6 hours before; clear drinks until 2 hours (P6) | ASA 2023: "The task force reaffirms the 2017 recommendations for clear liquids until 2 h preoperatively" [S9]. | Confirmed. |
| Bring your CPAP machine and mask | AACE 2019 R44: patients on CPAP should restart it "as early as the postanesthesia care unit" [S1]. | Confirmed. |
| Medicines list; loose clothes; leave valuables | Expert opinion. | Unchanged. |

## Bearing on open decisions

| Decision | Finding |
|---|---|
| P1 (diet length) | ERAS 2–4 weeks; Saudi SCFHS 2–6 weeks; ASMBS patient FAQ usually 2–3 weeks. |
| P2 (smoking) | ERAS: at least 4 weeks. ASMBS 2021 and AACE 2013: at least 6 weeks. AACE 2019: stop at any time, even within 6 weeks, since a meta-analysis did not support a fixed cut-off [S1, S4]. |
| P3 (GLP-1) | Multi-society 2024: continue most; 24-hour liquid diet for higher risk [S2]. SPAQI 2025: continue; 24 hours without solids for everyone [S7]. UK 2025: continue without changes [S8]. Bariatric patients are often already on a liquid pre-surgery diet. |
| P4 (pregnancy) | AACE 2019 states 12–18 months as a guideline (Grade D) [S1]. |
| P5 (endoscopy) | IFSO 2025: "strongly considered" before surgery for all [S5]. ASMBS (as reported by IFSO-WGO 2022): routine endoscopy "is justifiable, even in asymptomatic patients, and should be done at the surgeon's discretion" [S6]. |
| P6 (sweet clear drinks) | ASA 2023: "We recommend healthy adults drink carbohydrate-containing clear liquids until 2 h before elective procedures" (strong recommendation, moderate evidence); carbohydrate drinks can raise blood sugar in diabetes [S9]. ERAS 2021: insufficient evidence in bariatric surgery (Ali). |

## Suggested changes (not made; for your approval)

1. Put AACE 2019 back on the guide as a source for the pregnancy, hormone-pill, fertility, sleep apnea and CPAP lines, and retag the pregnancy line as Guideline.
2. Make the hormone line specific: **"Estrogen pills are usually stopped before surgery: 1 cycle of birth control pills, or 3 weeks of hormone replacement."** (AACE 2019 R18).
3. Add to the smoking panel: **"Surgery soon? Stop now. It still helps."** (AACE 2019 R23).
