Robotic Surgery

Minimally invasive surgery using a robotic system, with a magnified 3D view and wristed instruments.

  • Magnified 3D view

    A high-definition, three-dimensional image of the operating field.

  • Wristed instruments

    Instruments that bend and rotate for fine, controlled movement.

  • Small incisions

    Minimally invasive access instead of one large opening.

  • Established operations

    Sleeve, bypass, hernia and gallbladder surgery, done robotically.

01

What it is

In robotic surgery, the surgeon controls precise instruments and a magnified 3D camera from a console, working through small incisions. Dr. Alhazmi is trained in robotic surgery, and in recent years performed nearly all of his operations robotically.

02

Who it may suit

Many bariatric, hernia, gallbladder and other abdominal operations can be done robotically. The best approach for you is discussed at your consultation.

03

What to expect

Small incisions and the same preparation and follow-up as the equivalent standard operation.
