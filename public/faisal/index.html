Faisal

Faisal is a private assistant used only by Dr. Alaa Alhazmi. It places and answers phone calls for him and helps with his reminders and calendar.

With Dr. Alaa's permission, Faisal reads his Google Contacts (read-only) to know who is calling and whom to call, and reads and adds events in his Google Calendar and Google Tasks. It is not offered to anyone else.
