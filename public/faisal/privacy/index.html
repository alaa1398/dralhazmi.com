Faisal privacy policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Faisal is a private assistant used only by Dr. Alaa Alhazmi. Only his own Google account signs in to it.

Google data it uses

  • Google Contacts (read-only): names and phone numbers, to name callers and to find whom to call.
  • Google Calendar: to read busy times and to add or change events Dr. Alaa approves.
  • Google Tasks: to read and update his reminders.

How it is used and kept

The data is used only to provide these features to Dr. Alaa. It is kept on his own private server and in his own Notion workspace. To do its work, Faisal passes only what a task needs to the services it runs on: Anthropic (Claude, to plan a call or reminder and to read files Dr. Alaa sends), ElevenLabs (to place and answer calls) and Notion (his records). It is not sold, not shared with anyone else and not used for advertising.

Faisal's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

People who call

When someone calls, Faisal answers and records their name, phone number and message so Dr. Alaa's team can respond. This information is used only to respond to the caller and arrange the care they ask for. Callers do not need to share health details; any health information shared is treated as sensitive personal data under Saudi Arabia's Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL) and used only to respond. Caller messages are kept only as long as needed to respond and follow up, then deleted, or sooner if the caller asks, unless the law requires keeping them.

Where it is processed

Anthropic, ElevenLabs and Notion are service providers that may process this information outside Saudi Arabia.

Your rights

Under the PDPL, callers can ask to see their information, get a copy, have it corrected or deleted, and withdraw their consent at any time, by WhatsApp to +966 50 970 4040 or by email below. If not satisfied with the response, they can file a complaint with the Saudi Data & AI Authority (SDAIA). The full notice for patients and visitors is the site privacy policy (بالعربية).

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
