Faisal privacy policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Faisal is a private assistant used only by Dr. Alaa Alhazmi. Only his own Google account signs in to it.

Google data it uses

  • Google Contacts (read-only): names and phone numbers, to name callers and to find whom to call.
  • Google Calendar: to read busy times and to add or change events Dr. Alaa approves.
  • Google Tasks: to read and update his reminders.

How it is used and kept

The data is used only to provide these features to Dr. Alaa. It is kept on his own private server and in his own Notion workspace. To do its work, Faisal passes only what a task needs to the services it runs on: Anthropic (Claude, to plan a call or reminder and to read files Dr. Alaa sends), ElevenLabs (to place and answer calls) and Notion (his records). It is not sold, not shared with anyone else and not used for advertising.

Faisal's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
